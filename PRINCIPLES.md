# Principles

## 1. Work outlives the agent

Canonical mission state belongs to deterministic software. Sessions and
providers can change without becoming the owner of the work.

## 2. Agents execute; Judgment proposes

Agents perform bounded work. Judgment proposes a next justified cognitive
move within a closed contract and has no authority.

## 3. Software owns the records

State transitions, identities, permissions and evidence records are validated
by deterministic software. Model confidence cannot change those rules.

## 4. Humans retain consequential authority

Policy applies authorized rules. Human Gates preserve explicit,
attributable decisions over consequential effects. An agent cannot approve
itself or manufacture a human decision.

## 5. Evidence keeps its limits

Source identity, version, freshness, coverage and unavailable reads remain
visible. Missing evidence is not a successful result.

## 6. Declarations are not verification

What an agent or tool claims remains distinct from what an identified
evaluator or observation supports.

## 7. Success has separate meanings

Execution success, mission success and proof are separate. A conclusion
proposal is not an automatic mission-completion event.

## 8. Authorization is enforced outside the prompt

Signed permissions / Task Leases, admission and replay decisions belong to
deterministic boundaries. Provider credentials remain outside the agent.

## 9. Recovery preserves execution identity

A crash must not silently create a second execution. Uncertain effects require
visible reconciliation.

## 10. Views do not create authority

My Work, Memory, Operational Context and Trace Canvas should remain coherent
views of recorded work. Displaying an event does not create proof or permission.

## 11. Connectors and models remain replaceable

No provider defines canonical mission meaning or authority. Changes must
expose degraded states and coverage limits.

## 12. Protected boundaries fail closed

Missing required identity, evidence, policy or authorization stops the
protected action. Blocking remains targeted to that boundary.

## 13. Public claims follow scoped evidence

Tests and component qualifications do not establish an integrated release.
[STATUS.md](STATUS.md) is the current claim boundary.

## 14. Publication and licensing remain explicit

This documentation root does not distribute the private implementation.
Public component scope and licences are defined separately; see
[RIGHTS.md](RIGHTS.md) and [INSTALLATION.md](INSTALLATION.md).
